How ClearMic Works

Unlike other platforms that charge you monthly subscriptions and force you to upload your personal recordings to their servers, ClearMic processes everything directly in your browser.

1. Local Processing

We use the Web Audio API. Your file is decoded locally using your device's CPU, guaranteeing zero data breaches.

2. Dynamic EQ

Our algorithm applies Highpass (removes hum/rumble) and Lowpass (removes hiss) filters to isolate human speech.

3. Studio Compression

We dynamically level the volume of the speech, bringing up quiet whispers and taming loud peaks.
