How ClearMic Works

Unlike traditional tools that force you to wait for slow server uploads, ClearMic runs a highly optimized Digital Signal Processing (DSP) engine entirely within your browser.

1. Local Decoding Your device's CPU decodes the audio locally, guaranteeing absolute privacy.
2. Frequency Filtering We apply real-time Highpass (removes rumble) and Lowpass (removes hiss) filters.
3. Dynamic Compression Levels out the human voice to meet professional podcast broadcasting standards.
4. Instant Export The cleaned stream is captured and packaged into a high-quality WAV file instantly.
